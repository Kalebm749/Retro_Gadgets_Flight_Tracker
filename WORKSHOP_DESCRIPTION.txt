Real-time ADS-B flight tracker that displays live aircraft on a radar scope with geographic airport maps from OpenStreetMap.

Features:
• Live aircraft positions from ADSB.FI and ADSB.LOL with automatic failover
• Radar display with range rings and detailed airport maps (runways, taxiways, buildings)
• Traffic list with callsign, status, distance, altitude, and speed
• Aircraft detail panel (registration, type, squawk, vertical rate, bearing)
• 4 zoom levels: 1, 3, 5, 10 NM
• Stick pan navigation to explore the full 10 NM area when zoomed in
• Aircraft trails, emergency squawk alerts (7500/7600/7700)
• Adaptive polling with rate-limit handling
• Settings saved to FlashMemory

Supported airports: KAPA, KDEN, KBJC, KFTG, KCOS, KASE (Colorado region)

Source code: https://github.com/Kalebm749/Retro_Gadgets_Flight_Tracker
